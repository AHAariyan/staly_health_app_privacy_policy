Statly · Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 October 2026 · App: Statly (Android, package com.statly.app)

In short: Statly shows your steps, sleep, heart rate and workouts from Health Connect, with goals, streaks and badges. It only reads your health data, and that data stays on your phone. It is never uploaded to us, sold, shared, or used for advertising. There is no account and no advertising. The app sends anonymous usage statistics and crash reports, which never contain your health data. You can turn them off at any time in Settings.

1. Who we are

Statly is an Android app published by an independent developer ("we", "us"). Contact details are at the end of this policy.

2. Health data the app reads, and where it stays

Statly uses Health Connect, Android's on-device store for health and fitness data. With your permission, Statly reads the following types:

Health Connect data typeWhat Statly uses it for
StepsYour daily step count, step goal, streaks and step badges
DistanceDistance badges, for example walking a marathon distance in a week
Floors climbedFloor badges
Sleep (sessions and stages)Last night's sleep, sleep stages, sleep trends and sleep badges
Heart rateHeart rate summaries
Resting heart rateYour resting heart rate and its trend, and the related badge
Exercise sessionsYour workouts and active minutes

Statly may also ask for two optional Health Connect permissions:

  • Access past data, so it can show history and streaks from before you installed it.
  • Access data in the background, so its home-screen widgets can stay up to date.

You choose which types to allow, and you can change or remove access at any time in Health Connect's settings. Statly never writes, changes or deletes data in Health Connect.

The health data Statly reads, and the results it works out from it (your daily totals, streaks and earned badges), are processed and stored only on your device. They are not sent to us or to anyone else, and we have no way to see them.

3. How we use health data (Limited Use)

Statly's use of information received from Health Connect follows the Google Play policy for Android health permissions (Health Connect), including its Limited Use requirements. In particular, health data from Health Connect:

  • is used only to provide the features you see in the app: today's view, trends, goals, streaks, badges and widgets;
  • is never sold, and never transferred to anyone, including advertisers, data brokers and information resellers;
  • is never used for advertising, and never used to determine credit-worthiness or for lending purposes;
  • is never read by a human, and never used to train AI or machine-learning models.

4. Usage statistics and crash reports

To learn which features help, where people get stuck and why the app crashes, Statly uses Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics, both provided by Google. This is on by default. You can turn it off at any time in Settings → "Share anonymous usage stats".

These reports record what you do in the app, never your health data. Examples: that the Welcome screen was shown, that Health Connect was connected, that a badge was earned (only the badge's name), that the Pro screen was opened, or that a purchase succeeded or was cancelled. They never contain step counts, sleep times, heart rates, workouts or any other value read from Health Connect.

Firebase also collects standard technical information: device model, Android version, app version, country, and a random app-instance identifier. Crash reports contain the technical state of the app when it crashed. Google processes this data under its own privacy policy and the Firebase privacy information.

5. Purchases

Statly Pro is sold through Google Play. Google handles the payment, and we never see your card or payment details. The app only learns whether you own Pro, so it can unlock it.

6. What we do not do

  • No account or sign-in. We don't ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data.
  • No location tracking.

7. Keeping and deleting your data

Everything Statly stores lives in the app's private storage on your phone. To delete it, clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall the app. Your original data stays in Health Connect, which you control.

Usage statistics are kept by Google Analytics for up to 14 months. You can ask us to delete statistics linked to your app-instance identifier by contacting us.

8. Children

Statly is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change how Statly handles data, we will update this page and its date before the change ships. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
